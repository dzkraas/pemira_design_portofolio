# pemira_design_portofolio
Design portfolio for my role as the Head of PDD Division at Pemira 2025.

As the Head of the PDD Division, I was responsible for managing publications, documentation, and visual design to support the execution of the Student Association General Election.

I led the planning and production of digital publication materials, ensuring that election-related information was clear, well-structured, and visually engaging. I coordinated documentation activities and collaborated with other divisions to ensure consistent communication and timely information delivery to students.

This role strengthened my skills in visual communication, data communication, UI/UX thinking, content organization, and teamwork, particularly in presenting important information in a user-centered and accessible way.
